# SUPERSTORE SALES DASHBOARD (Interactive Dashboard using MS Excel)

---

## PROJECT OBJECTIVES

- Analyze sales and profit performance.
- Monitor key business KPIs.
- Compare sales across different regions and categories.
- Identify top-performing products and sub-categories.
- Analyze monthly sales trends.
- Support business decision-making through data visualization.

## Questions (KPIs)

- What is the total sales generated?
- What is the total profit?
- What is the average sales?
- How many orders were placed?
- How many customers are there?
- Which region has the highest sales?
- Which category contributes the most to sales?
- What are the monthly sales trends?
- Which sub-categories have the highest sales?
- How do sales vary across regions and categories?
- Which shipping mode has the highest number of orders?

## Project Insights

- The dashboard analyzes sales, profit, orders, and customer data.
- The West region has the highest sales performance.
- Technology is the highest-selling category.
- Chairs and Phones are among the top-performing sub-categories.
- Monthly sales trends help identify high and low sales periods.
- Standard Class has the highest number of orders among the shipping modes.
- The dashboard provides an interactive view of overall Superstore sales performance.
